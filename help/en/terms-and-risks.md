# Terms of service and your account

Please read this before you use Labs Studio with Google Flow.

**Using software to drive Google Flow or ChatGPT through their websites is against those services' consumer terms of service.** Their consumer terms restrict using automated means to access the services or extract their output, outside the ways they offer for that, such as their official APIs. Read them yourself: [Google Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms) and [OpenAI Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use). Labs Studio's Google Flow pages, and the google-flow MCP server used by your own agent, work the Flow website in a browser on your behalf. That is this kind of automated use. (Labs Studio has no ChatGPT web feature today. The same applies if one is added.)

What this means for you:

- **The risk is to your own account.** Google or OpenAI can limit, suspend or close an account they believe breaks their terms. That can include the subscription you pay for and, for a Google account, other Google services on it. Labs Studio can't protect you from that, and nobody can promise it won't happen.
- **Use an account you can afford to lose**, and never someone else's.
- **You are responsible** for what you generate and for following the terms of the services you use.

What Labs Studio does to keep usage close to a person working by hand. These measures lower the load on the site; they don't make the use allowed:

- You sign in yourself. The app never types, sees or stores your password.
- It never tries to get around a CAPTCHA, a "verify it's you" check or a usage limit. It stops and shows you the window.
- It runs one job at a time, waits a random gap between jobs, and stops at a daily cap ([Signing in to Google Flow](google-flow-sign-in.md)).
- It uses one account per site.

**The safe alternative** is the official APIs, which are made for automated use. They need your own keys and are billed per use, and Labs Studio doesn't use them.
